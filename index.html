About

My research interests lie at the intersection of corporate finance and market microstructure, focusing on the trading of bond ETFs, corporate bonds, market efficiency, liquidity, and trading behavior.

I teach Corporate Financial Management, Investment Analysis, and the River Hawk Fund, a student-managed investment fund.
